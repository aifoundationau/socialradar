3. THE REDISTRIBUTIVE EQUITY LEDGER & REVENUE BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 AUSTRALIAN DOLLAR (AUD) BASE CURRENCY STANDARD:
   All financial transactions, Stripe Checkout charges, and official tax invoices 
   are settled exclusively in Australian Dollars (AUD). International prices in 
   US Dollars (USD) are displayed for transparency using the live Frankfurter 
   European Central Bank (ECB) reference rate, but the accounting ledger 
   operates strictly in AUD whole cents and whole tokens.

3.2 REVENUE POOL INGESTION:
   Whenever a customer completes a token pack purchase or service order on Stripe:
   a. The gross receipt in AUD cents is captured in `stripe_purchases`.
   b. Corresponding integer tokens are minted to the customer's wallet.
   c. The transaction is instantly indexed into the Master Order Ledger.

3.3 MATHEMATICAL BREAKDOWN OF REVENUE DISTRIBUTION:
   The AdRadarHQ platform engine executes deterministic, zero-decimal arithmetic 
   (`Math.round`, no floating-point fractions) across the active ledger:

   1. TOTAL POOL ACCUMULATION:
      - Total Gross AUD Revenue = Sum of all AUD cents from Stripe purchases.
      - Total Tokens Pool = Sum of all tokens issued across purchases.

   2. MAIN ADMIN & EQUITY PARTNER ALLOCATION:
      - Each active partner (including the Main Admin's designated partner ID) 
        holds an assigned contractual percentage (P%).
      - Partner AUD Entitlement = Math.round(Total Gross AUD Revenue * (P / 100))
      - Partner Token Entitlement = Math.round(Total Tokens Pool * (P / 100))
      - Partner USD Indicative Value = Math.round(Total Gross USD Revenue * (P / 100))

   3. PLATFORM TREASURY RETAINED SHARE:
      - Treasury Retained Percentage = 100% - Sum of all Active Partner Percentages.
      - Treasury AUD Reserve = Math.round(Total Gross AUD Revenue * (Treasury % / 100))
      - Treasury Token Reserve = Math.round(Total Tokens Pool * (Treasury % / 100))
      - The Treasury Reserve covers server infrastructure, API costs, Gemini LLM 
        inference, Stripe processing overheads, and corporate reserves.

3.4 AUDITABILITY & TRANSPARENCY:
   The Main Admin has complete visibility over order history, token consumption, 
   and client purchase volume. Real-time partner earnings and pool distributions 
   are reflected in the Redistributive Ledger view under strict cryptographic 
   audit trails.

4. TAXATION, COMPLIANCE, AND AUSTRALIAN CONSUMER LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 GOODS & SERVICES TAX (GST):
   a. Australian Orders: Subject to 10% Australian GST, calculated exclusively 
      and added on top via Stripe Automatic Tax.
   b. New Zealand & International Orders: 0% GST (Zero Tax). New Zealand orders 
      do not attract Australian GST.

4.2 STATUTORY 7-YEAR AUDIT RETENTION:
   In strict compliance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) and the 
   Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), all customer transactions, token debits, 
   admin replies, and customer refund records are preserved in immutable 
   7-year archival storage. Neither Party may purge or delete statutory logs.

4.3 REFUND PROTOCOL:
   Customer refund requests must be submitted in writing via the in-app plain-text 
   messaging system and evaluated under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL). 
   Approved refunds restore tokens to the client wallet and adjust the 
   Redistributive Ledger proportionally.
