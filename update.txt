WHAT WE JUST UPGRADED ACROSS ALL AD SEARCHES (META, TIKTOK, GOOGLE & YOUTUBE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Previously, searching for ads on Meta, TikTok, or Google only did a basic lookup
that often returned brand-new test ads, incomplete cards (missing headlines or
descriptions), or loose keyword matches.

We upgraded all three platforms into smart, multi-engine "Winner Intelligence"
systems so AdRadarHQ automatically finds the highest-performing, longest-running,
and most profitable ads on each platform.


1. STRICT PLATFORM SEPARATION (NO MIXING OF PLATFORMS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Select META: You ONLY get Facebook, Instagram, Reels, Stories, and Messenger ads.
- Select TIKTOK: You ONLY get TikTok ads.
- Select GOOGLE: You ONLY get Google Search, Google Shopping, Google Display,
  and YouTube ads.
- Clicking a different platform tab now immediately clears the old platform's
  results and runs the search for the platform you selected.


2. HOW TIKTOK SEARCH WAS IMPROVED (DUAL-ENGINE SYSTEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Engine 1 (TikTok Commercial Ad Library): Searches exact commercial phrases
  sorted by highest audience reach (most views) across up to 4 pages, instead
  of stopping after 1 page.
- Engine 2 (TikTok Creative Center Top Ads): Connects to TikTok Creative Center
  Top Ads (when APIFY_API_TOKEN is added) to pull ads ranked in the Top 1%–10%
  for Click-Through Rate (CTR) in Australia, New Zealand, the US, UK, and Canada.
- Viral Velocity Scoring: Ads that rack up thousands of views per day quickly
  are now scored alongside long-running 28+ and 60+ day ads.
- Automatic Format Labels: Every TikTok ad is automatically labeled by its
  winning style (e.g., "Creator Review & Unboxing", "Problem-Agitate-Solve",
  "TikTok Made Me Buy It") and given a clear headline and button.


3. HOW GOOGLE & YOUTUBE SEARCH WAS IMPROVED (3-ENGINE SYSTEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Engine 1 (Live Google Search & Shopping Ads): Checks live Google Search results
  in your selected country to capture the actual Sponsored Text Ads and Google
  Shopping product ads paying to sit at the very top of Google right now.
- Engine 2 (Multi-Brand Google Transparency Center): Instead of checking only 1
  website when you search a category (like "running shoes" or "car"), it now
  checks up to 3 top competing brands at the same time to pull their longest-
  running YouTube Video Ads, Display Banners, and Search Ads.
- Engine 3 (Full Ad Details & YouTube Previews): Automatically fills in the
  missing headlines, descriptions, Call-To-Action buttons, and YouTube video
  links for the top winners so Google Ad cards never show up blank.


4. HOW META (FACEBOOK & INSTAGRAM) SEARCH WAS IMPROVED (DUAL-ENGINE SYSTEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Exact-Phrase + Broad Search: Multi-word searches (like "protein powder" or
  "teeth whitening") now search for the exact phrase first so you get real
  product ads instead of unrelated posts that happen to share one word.
- Ad-Set Scaling Detection: Tracks how many copies of the same ad a brand is
  running at once ("5 Ad Sets Scaling") and how many total ads that Facebook
  Page is running—showing you which brands are spending serious money.
- Full Carousel & Reels Details: Pulls text from all Carousel cards (not just
  the first card), captures discount/shipping notes, and uses Facebook Page
  likes and placement data (Facebook + Instagram + Reels) to rank the best
  performing ads at the top.


5. SMARTER AI REMIX STUDIO FOR EACH PLATFORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- When you click "Remix" on an ad, the AI now adapts its strategy to the exact
  platform:
  * Meta Ads: Focuses on the 3-second Reels/Feed visual hook, "above-the-fold"
    caption, and Carousel offer stack.
  * TikTok Ads: Focuses on the 2-second thumbstop, native UGC creator style,
    and on-screen text captions.
  * Google & YouTube Ads: Focuses on high-intent Google Search headlines,
    sitelinks, and the first 5 seconds of a YouTube video (before the "Skip Ad"
    button appears).
- Designed to run smoothly and reliably on all AI models, including fast,
  low-cost models (Gemini Flash-Lite, Gemini 3.8 Flash Low, Claude Haiku, and
  gpt-6-luna).
