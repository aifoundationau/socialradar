WHAT IS THE REDISTRIBUTIVE EQUITY LEDGER?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Think of the Redistributive Equity Ledger as an automatic, transparent "profit 
and token-sharing system" built right into the platform.

Whenever a customer buys tokens or a subscription through Stripe, money comes 
into the platform and tokens are minted. 

Instead of hiding this revenue or calculating profit splits manually on a 
spreadsheet at the end of the month, the Redistributive Equity Ledger tracks 
every single dollar and token in real-time and automatically calculates who owns 
what share of the business pie.


HOW DOES IT WORK? (STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE MONEY POOL
   Every time someone completes a purchase through Stripe:
   - The platform records the purchase.
   - It adds the tokens sold to the "Total Tokens Pool".
   - It adds the money collected to the "Total Revenue Pool" (calculated in both 
     Australian Dollars and US Dollars).

2. EQUITY PARTNERS
   The SuperAdmin can list partners (co-founders, investors, or team members) in 
   the SuperAdmin Panel and assign each partner a percentage.
   
   For example:
   - Founder / Partner A: 20%
   - Partner B: 10%
   - Combined Partners Total: 30%

3. AUTOMATIC DISTRIBUTION
   The system instantly and automatically calculates what each partner has 
   earned:
   - Partner A gets 20% of all tokens and 20% of all revenue.
   - Partner B gets 10% of all tokens and 10% of all revenue.
   All token numbers are strictly rounded to whole numbers (no fractions).

4. THE TREASURY (WHAT REMAINS IN THE BUSINESS)
   Whatever percentage is not assigned to partners automatically stays in the 
   business. This is called the "Treasury" (or Platform Reserve).
   
   If partners have 30% total, the Treasury automatically holds the remaining 
   70% of all tokens and revenue to pay for server costs, API usage, marketing, 
   and business savings.


AN EASY EXAMPLE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Imagine the platform has made 10 sales totaling:
- Total Revenue: $1,000 AUD
- Total Tokens Sold: 1,000 Tokens

If you set up:
- Sarah: 15%
- Mark: 10%

The ledger instantly shows:
- Sarah's Share:    150 Tokens  |  $150.00 AUD
- Mark's Share:     100 Tokens  |  $100.00 AUD
- Platform Treasury: 750 Tokens |  $750.00 AUD (the remaining 75%)


KEY FEATURES OF THIS SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 100% Real-Time & Live:
   No waiting for end-of-month reconciliations. Every time a Stripe payment lands, 
   all numbers update in seconds.

2. Dual Currency Transparency:
   It tracks everything in Australian Dollars (AUD) and automatically displays 
   the equivalent in US Dollars (USD) so international partners always know 
   their balance.

3. SuperAdmin Control:
   Only the SuperAdmin has permission to view, add, edit, or adjust equity 
   partners. Regular users and operational admins cannot see or change this.

4. Safe "Refresh to Zero" (New Payout Period):
   When it's time to pay out partners or close an accounting cycle, the SuperAdmin 
   can click "Refresh Ledger to 0". 
   This does NOT delete financial history. It safely packages up all past 
   transactions into an immutable 7-year audit archive, and resets the live 
   dashboard to zero so you can start the next period clean.


IN SHORT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Redistributive Equity Ledger is your automated bookkeeper that fairly, 
openly, and instantly divides company revenue and tokens between business 
partners and the company treasury.
