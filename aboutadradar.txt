1. ADRADAR IN ONE MINUTE
================================================================================
AdRadar finds the ads that are already winning, shows you why they work, and
rewrites them for your brand.

- SEARCH real ads from the official ad libraries of Meta (Facebook and
  Instagram), TikTok and Google (Search, Shopping, Display and YouTube).
- FILTER to "proven winners": ads that have kept running for 28 days or more
  (or 60+ days), because advertisers don't keep paying for ads that lose money.
- SCORE every ad from 0 to 100 on how well it fits your brand, how strong its
  hook is, and how long and widely it has run.
- REMIX: AI breaks a winning ad down (hook, pacing, persuasion framework, why
  it works) and writes three ready-to-test versions for YOUR brand, each with
  a headline, call to action, ad copy, a shot-by-shot video storyboard and an
  image prompt.
- GET HUMAN HELP when you want it: the "Customize" service sends your brief to
  a real Ad Agent near you, who sends back a full brand and ad strategy.
- PAY AS YOU GO with tokens (default 1 token = AUD $1). No retainer, no
  lock-in contract, and tokens never expire.

AdRadar is built by AI Foundation Australia. Its mission, in the founder's
words: "to level the playing field for ambitious entrepreneurs, agency
operators, and businesses across Australia, New Zealand, and globally."

3. EVERYTHING ADRADAR DOES (ALL FUNCTIONS)
================================================================================

3.1 RADAR SEARCH (find winning ads)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Search by competitor brand, product or niche keyword, or a hook/phrase used
  in ads (2 to 100 characters).
    * Meta and TikTok search the words in the ads and the advertiser's name.
      Phrases of several words are searched as an exact phrase first, then
      broadened when few ads come back.
    * Google is searched by advertiser: a brand name or website (for example
      nike.com) works best. Common categories (cars, pets, skincare,
      supplements, running shoes and activewear, teeth whitening) are
      recognised and mapped to the leading advertisers in that category. Live
      Google sponsored search and Shopping ads are included too.
- Choose the ad library: Meta, TikTok or Google. Results never mix
  platforms ("strict platform isolation").
- Choose the country (each library is searched for that market).
- Choose the format: all, video or image.
- Winner filters:
    * Proven (28+ days): only ads that have run 28 days or longer.
    * Extended (60+ days): evergreen ads running two months or longer.
    * Top Winners (90%+): only ads scoring 90 or more out of 100.
- Pick one of your brands so results are scored for fit with that brand.
- Winner Matrix score (0-100) for every ad: 40% brand fit, 30% hook strength,
  30% longevity and scale (days running, duplicate ad sets, estimated views).
  Each card shows the score, why the ad resonates, and highlighted keywords.
- Ad cards show the creative (image, video or carousel), copy, headline, call
  to action, landing page link, days running and estimated views/likes.
- Results are kept for 24 hours, so repeat searches come back instantly.
- Clear on-screen notices when a library has limits (see section 11).

3.2 AI CREATIVE REMIX STUDIO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Model this ad": the AI (Google Gemini) watches or reads the winning ad and
  produces a DECONSTRUCTION:
    * Hook analysis: the pattern interrupt in the first 0-3 seconds.
    * Pacing structure: beat by beat, with timings.
    * Persuasion framework: PAS (problem-agitate-solve), AIDA, UGC story,
      us-vs-them, or social proof stack.
    * Why it works: the psychology in plain words.
- Then THREE VARIATIONS written for your brand profile, each with a different
  angle: name of the angle, headline, call-to-action button, primary ad copy,
  a 4-6 beat video storyboard (shot, voiceover line, on-screen text) and an
  image-generation prompt for a static ad.
- Platform-aware writing: TikTok-native UGC hooks, Google search/YouTube
  pre-skip hooks, or Meta Reels/Feed/Stories structure.
- It never copies the source ad's brand names, claims or wording, and it
  doesn't invent prices, statistics, reviews or guarantees your brand profile
  doesn't state.
- Regenerate fresh variations, edit them, copy the full script in one click,
  or export a CSV spreadsheet for editors and UGC creators.
- If the AI fails, the tokens are automatically re-credited.

3.4 CUSTOMIZE BRAND STRATEGY (human experts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The brief is routed to the closest active Ad Agent by location (worked out

- You get an instant confirmation in your Message Panel naming your Ad Agent,
  their phone number, their location and the distance from you.
- Your Ad Agent reviews your positioning, audience, pain points and notes and
  delivers a customized brand and ad strategy back to your Message Panel.
  Agents have an AI draft assistant that structures replies in four parts:
  positioning and offer refinement, audience and pain-point hooks, three
  custom ad creative blueprints, and an implementation checklist.
- Fixed fee per request (default 55 tokens), not a retainer.

5. ROLES: WHO CAN DO WHAT
================================================================================
Every account has one or more roles. A person can hold several at once (for
example Supporter + Ad Agent + Agency); they then get everything each role
allows. Their main displayed role follows this order: Super Admin > Main Admin
> Ad Agent > Moderator > Agency > Supporter.

SUPPORTER (everyone starts here)
  - Search the Radar, use the AI Remix Studio, manage brands, clone community
    brands, order Customize, buy tokens, see wallet history, message support.

AGENCY
  - Same tools as Supporter, set up for teams managing several client brands
    (account tier "agency"). Often combined with Ad Agent.

AD AGENT (independent contractor)
  - Everything a Supporter can do, plus the Ad Agent Panel: profile and
    availability, assigned Customize orders, AI draft replies, sending
    strategies to customers, contractors directory, own ledger line.
  - Earns a redistribution percentage on the customizations they handle.
  - Ad Agents are independent contractors, not employees, partners or legal
    representatives of AdRadar; their advice is their own.

MODERATOR
  - Searches and remixes for quality checks; helps with support
    communication, FAQ accuracy and Community Library quality. Cannot change
    prices, tokens, ledgers or roles.

MAIN ADMIN
  - The Admin Control Panel, within what the Role Permissions grid allows:
    pricing and token packs, FAQs, messages, analytics, the user list and
    (if allowed) approving roles.
  - Can approve Supporter, Ad Agent, Agency and Moderator. Cannot make
    anyone Main Admin or Super Admin, cannot change the permissions grid,
    and cannot gift or remove tokens or run the free-access windows.

SUPER ADMIN (the platform owner; permanent)
  - Everything, always. The only role that can: assign Main Admin, edit the
    permissions grid, gift/remove/reset tokens and refund charges, run Timed
    Free Access Windows, manage the Ad Agent Distribution Ledger and payouts,
    and permanently delete messages.
  - The Super Admin account is fixed to the database owner's verified email
    and can't be changed or demoted by anyone.

THE ROLE PERMISSIONS GRID
  Super Admin decides, per role, which of these 10 permissions it has:
    View Analytics . System Config . Redistributive Ledger . Flush Cache .
    Manage Messages . Manage RBAC (approve roles) . Manage Users .
    Search AdRadarHQ . AI Remix Studio . Manage Brands
  The server enforces the grid. It can only narrow what a role can do: it
  never opens admin screens to non-admin roles. For example, unticking
  "Search AdRadarHQ" for Supporters stops Supporters searching; ticking
  "Manage Messages" for Ad Agents does NOT give them the admin inbox.


================================================================================
6. HOW ROLES ARE ALLOCATED
================================================================================
1. Sign in with Google. Every new account is a SUPPORTER automatically.
2. SUPER ADMIN is not assigned: it belongs permanently to the platform
   owner's verified account.
3. Super Admin (or a Main Admin who has the "Manage RBAC" permission) opens
   Users and Roles, finds the person by email, and ticks the roles they
   should have. Several roles can be ticked at once.
4. Becoming an AD AGENT is a two-step process:
     a) Approval: Super Admin (or an authorised Main Admin) gives the person
        the Ad Agent role. Supporters can't make themselves agents.
     b) Registration: the new agent fills in the Ad Agent profile (name,
        phone, location, bank/PayID). Until then the app keeps prompting
        them. Once saved and switched ON, they appear in the agent directory
        and start receiving nearby Customize orders.
   Super Admin can also add an agent directly in the ledger, with their
   redistribution percentage (10%, 20% ... 70%; default 30%).
5. Changes apply fast:
     - A promotion takes effect within seconds (the app refreshes the
       person's login automatically).
     - A demotion takes effect immediately: the person is signed out and
       signs back in with the reduced role.
6. Main Admin can never create another Main Admin; only Super Admin can.

7. MONEY FLOWS: THE TWO REDISTRIBUTION LEDGERS
================================================================================
AdRadar shares what it earns, and shows it openly.

7.1 REDISTRIBUTIVE EQUITY LEDGER (partners)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Every Stripe purchase adds its tokens and money (AUD, with a USD view) to
  the pool, in real time.
- Super Admin lists equity partners (co-founders, investors, team members)
  and each one's percentage. Active partners can't add up to more than 100%.
- Each partner's share is calculated instantly; whatever isn't allocated
  stays in the Treasury (the platform reserve for servers, APIs, marketing and
  savings).
- Example: AUD $1,000 of sales with partners at 15% and 10% gives AUD $150,
  AUD $100, and AUD $750 to the Treasury.
- "Refresh Ledger to 0" starts a new payout period. Past purchases are
  archived for 7 years, not deleted.
- Tokens Super Admin gifts never count as revenue in this ledger.

7.2 AD AGENT DISTRIBUTION LEDGER (agents)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Every Customize order handled by an agent adds what the customer paid for
  it to that agent's total.
- The agent earns their redistribution percentage (10-70%, default 30%) of
  what's owed since their last payout.
- Super Admin pays agents (bank or PayID) and resets their amount to 0. That
  marks everything up to that moment as paid, even if the agent's percentage
  changes later.

Why this matters for marketing: Ad Agents earn more when they bring in and
serve Customize customers, so every agent is also a local promoter.

8.1 THE PROBLEM WITH THE USUAL OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Big agencies:
  - Monthly retainers, minimum terms and management fees, before a single
    ad is tested.
  - Slow briefs and approval loops; small accounts get junior staff.
  - Their research and swipe files stay theirs; when you leave, so does the
    knowledge.
Facebook-ad gurus and "marketing agencies" that sell books, courses and
coaching:
  - You pay for opinions and frameworks written months or years ago, for a
    platform that changes every week.
  - The real product is often the next upsell (course, mastermind, "done for
    you" package).
  - Generic advice, not built around your product, audience or offer.

8.2 WHAT ADRADAR DOES DIFFERENTLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Evidence, not opinion. AdRadar shows real ads from the official ad
   libraries. The Proven (28+ days) and Extended (60+ days) filters keep only
   ads advertisers have kept paying for. That is the market's own verdict on
   what works.
2. Current, not out of date. You see what is running now, in your country,
   on each platform, not a case study from a book.
3. It teaches as it works. Every remix explains the hook, pacing, persuasion
   framework and psychology of the winning ad. You learn the skill by using
   it; no book required.
4. Built for your brand. Variations are written from your own brand profile
   (audience, pain points, offer, tone), never a generic template.
5. Minutes, not weeks. Search, deconstruct and get three ready-to-test
   scripts with storyboards in a few minutes.
6. Pay only for what you use. From AUD $1 a token, no retainer, no lock-in,
   no cancellation penalties, and tokens never expire.
7. Every cent is visible. Your wallet history shows each token you spend;

8. Human experts when you want them, at a fixed price. A Customize request

9. All the main platforms in one place: Meta, TikTok and Google/YouTube.
10. You keep everything: copy the scripts or export them to CSV and use them

11. Local and fair. Australian pricing in AUD with GST handled correctly,

Start small and test: a modest monthly paid budget split between Meta,

the top performer each fortnight and replace the rest. Watch cost per
paying customer, not just clicks.

11. HONEST LIMITS (WHAT NOT TO PROMISE)
================================================================================
- TikTok: TikTok's official ad library only covers Europe and the UK. For
  Australia, the US and other countries, TikTok results come from UK ads (the
  app says so on screen).
- Google: the Transparency Center is searched by advertiser, so brand names
  and websites work best; random keywords may find nothing.
- Views and likes on Meta and Google ads are estimates.
- A search that runs but finds no ads still costs its tokens. Encourage good
  searches (brand names, product keywords, real hook phrases).
- The AI writes ready-to-test drafts, not guaranteed winners; always test
  and check claims before publishing.
- AdRadar shows other advertisers' public ads for research. Customers must
  create their own ads and not copy logos, trademarks or exact wording.
- The Monthly Subscription is off by default and currently admin-only.
