================================================================================
                  ADRADARHQ — RBAC ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES GUIDE
================================================================================

This document explains what each Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) role does in
AdRadarHQ, which permissions it receives by default in `system_config/rbac`,
which workspace panels it unlocks, and how Multi-Role Assignment combines
capabilities when a user holds multiple roles simultaneously.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SUPPORTER (`supporter`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Default Role for All Registered Customers & Platform Members.

Default RBAC Permissions:
  - `search_radar`   : Run live ad radar searches across Meta, TikTok, and Google
                       (including Proven Winners 28+d, Extended 60+d, and 90%+
                       Top Winners filters).
  - `ai_remix`       : Use the AI Creative Remix Studio to deconstruct ads,
                       generate 3 hook variants, storyboards, and regenerate
                       fresh script variations.
  - `manage_brands`  : Create, edit, and manage personal Brand Profiles, clone
                       community cards from the Global Brand Library, and submit
                       "Customize Your Brand Strategy with Professional Experts"
                       requests.

Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
  - Access the **Supporter Panel** (Account Details, Personal Token Balance,
    Order History, and Direct Message to Super Admin / Ad Agents).
  - Purchase Token Packs (AUD with 10% GST in Australia, AUD in NZ, USD
    internationally) and spend tokens on Radar Searches, AI Remix, Global Brand
    Clones, and Professional Customize Service orders.
  - Select or get matched with the closest registered **Ad Agent** by geolocation
    when ordering a Professional Customize Service.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AD AGENT (`ad_agent`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Professional Advertising & Brand Strategy Specialist Who Handles Customer
Customization Orders and Earns Redistribution Payouts.

Default RBAC Permissions:
  - `manage_messages`: View, reply to, and manage customer customization briefs
                       and direct messages assigned to the Ad Agent.
  - `search_radar`   : Full access to Ad Radar intelligence searches.
  - `ai_remix`       : Full access to AI Creative Remix Studio for client work.
  - `manage_brands`  : Full access to Brand Profiles and Global Brand Library.

Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
  - Access the dedicated **Ad Agent Panel** in the top navigation bar.
  - Register and update their **Ad Agent Profile** (Name, Phone, Suburb, State,
    Geolocation coordinates, and Specialties) so customers are matched to the
    closest Ad Agent via Haversine distance calculation.
  - Receive and fulfill **Professional Customize Service** orders submitted by
    customers (delivering tailored brand strategy replies and ad variations).
  - Appear in the **Ad Agent Distribution Ledger** (`LEDGER` tab), where:
      * Every completed customization order adds the live **Professional
        Customize Service** token/currency value to their **Customizations Total**.
      * They earn a configurable **Redistribution Percentage** (`10%`, `20%`,
        `30%`, `40%`, `50%`, `60%`, or `70%`) displayed in **Redist. Amount**.
      * Their **Bank Details / PayID Details** are stored for Super Admin payout
        invoices (`.txt` download) and balance resets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. AGENCY (`agency`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commercial / Multi-Brand Agency Tier Account for High-Volume Teams & Clients.

Default RBAC Permissions:
  - `search_radar`   : Run high-volume competitive ad intelligence searches.
  - `ai_remix`       : Generate multi-angle creative deconstructions and scripts
                       across multiple client brands.
  - `manage_brands`  : Manage multiple client Brand Profiles, copy Global Brand
                       Library cards, and commission Professional Customizations.

Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
  - Sets the user's account tier to `agency` (`tier: "agency"`).
  - Designed for marketing agencies, media buyers, and creative teams managing
    multiple brand portfolios, competitive research, and ad remix workflows.
  - Can be combined with `Supporter` and `Ad Agent` so an agency account can
    simultaneously manage client brands, fulfill customization requests as a
    registered Ad Agent, and use Supporter wallet features.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MODERATOR (`moderator`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Platform Trust, Safety, Analytics & Communications Moderator.

Default RBAC Permissions:
  - `view_analytics` : View platform telemetry, search volume metrics, and
                       operational performance summaries.
  - `manage_messages`: Monitor, review, and respond to user inquiries and
                       support messages while respecting 7-year statutory
                       retention rules.
  - `search_radar`   : Run Radar searches to verify ad feed quality.
  - `ai_remix`       : Test and review AI Creative Remix outputs.

Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
  - Assist with customer support communications, FAQ/knowledge accuracy, and
    community Global Brand Library quality review.
  - Cannot modify Token Economy rates, Stripe Token Packs, Equity Partner splits,
    or RBAC role assignments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MAIN ADMIN (`admin`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Senior Platform Administrator with Access to the Admin Control Panel.

Default RBAC Permissions:
  - `view_analytics` : Full access to platform analytics and ledger summaries.
  - `manage_config`  : Configure Currency & Token Economy toggles, Service
                       Pricing (including the **Professional Customize Service**
                       token price and **Monthly Search Subscription** fee),
                       Token Packs CRUD, and Help/FAQ items.
  - `manage_messages`: Read, compose, copy, and manage all user and Ad Agent
                       communications in the Admin Messages inbox.
  - `search_radar`   : Full Radar search access + eligible for the privileged
                       1,000 searches/month subscription tier.
  - `ai_remix`       : Full AI Creative Remix Studio access.
  - `manage_brands`  : Full Brand Profile and Global Brand Library management.

Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
  - Access the **Admin Control Panel** (`Currency Controls`, `Service Pricing`,
    `Token Packs`, `Token Controls & Ledger`, `User Messages`, and `Help & FAQs`).
  - Adjust the **Professional Customize Service** price in **Service Pricing**,
    which automatically updates the **Customizations Total** and **Redist. Amount**
    in the **Ad Agent Distribution Ledger**.
  - Note: Certain destructive or sovereign controls (such as assigning RBAC
    roles, resetting Ad Agent / Equity Partner ledgers to 0, or gifting/removing
    user tokens) are reserved exclusively for **Super Admin**.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SUPER ADMIN (`superadmin` — Permanent Sovereign Account)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Highest-Assurance Sovereign Owner (`david@aifoundation.com.au` / `SUPERADMIN_EMAIL`).

Default RBAC Permissions (Permanently Locked — All 9 Permissions):
  - `manage_rbac`    : Assign single or multiple RBAC roles to any user and
                       configure the live RBAC permission matrix.
  - `manage_equity`  : Add/remove Equity Partners, update equity splits, and
                       manage the **Ad Agent Distribution Ledger** (auto-fill
                       registered Ad Agents, save/copy/hover Bank & PayID
                       details, set 10%–70% redistribution, download `.txt`
                       payout invoices, and confirm-reset balances to `0`).
  - `gift_tokens`    : Add, minus, reset, or refund tokens for any user account.
  - Plus all other permissions (`view_analytics`, `manage_config`,
    `manage_messages`, `search_radar`, `ai_remix`, `manage_brands`).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. MULTI-ROLE STACKING EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a user is assigned:
  [☑ Supporter]  [☑ Ad Agent]  [☑ Agency]  [☐ Moderator]  [☐ Main Admin]

How AdRadarHQ Evaluates Their Combined Roles:
  1. **Stored Roles Array (`roles`)**: `["ad_agent", "agency", "supporter"]`
  2. **Primary Display Role (`role`)**: `"ad_agent"` (determined by priority
     order: `superadmin > admin > ad_agent > moderator > agency > supporter`)
  3. **Account Tier (`tier`)**: `"agency"` (unlocked by the `Agency` role)
  4. **Effective Permissions**: Union of all permissions granted to `Supporter`,
     `Ad Agent`, and `Agency` (`manage_messages`, `search_radar`, `ai_remix`,
     and `manage_brands`).
  5. **Unlocked Workspace Panels**:
     - Can open both the **Ad Agent Panel** (to manage their geolocation,
       customization orders, and Bank/PayID payout details) AND the **Supporter
       Panel** (to view their personal wallet, agency brand profiles, and order
       history).
