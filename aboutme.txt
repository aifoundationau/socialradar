1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & MISSION
================================================================================
AdRadarHQ is a high-performance competitor ad intelligence and AI creative
remix web application designed to level the playing field for ambitious
entrepreneurs, agency operators, and businesses across Australia, New Zealand,
and globally.

Traditional advertising agencies charge massive monthly retainers ($3,000 to
$10,000+ per month) with lengthy lock-in contracts, slow turnaround times, and
untested creative theories. "Guru" courses sell outdated books and templates
that stop working as ad algorithms evolve.

AdRadarHQ flips the model completely:
1. Real Proven Winners: Search the live, official ad libraries of Meta
   (Facebook & Instagram), TikTok, and Google (Search, Shopping, Display,
   and YouTube) to see what is already working in the market right now.
2. Longevity as the Profit Proxy: Advertisers do not keep paying for ads that
   lose money. If an ad has run for 28+ days or 60+ days, it is converting.
3. AI Creative Remix Studio: Google Gemini deconstructs winning competitor ad
   psychology (hooks, pacing, persuasion frameworks) and writes 3 brand-new,
   ready-to-test creative variations tailored to your specific brand.
4. Human Professional Services: When you need expert custom creative strategy,
   AdRadarHQ connects you with certified Ad Agents located near you via
   intelligent geolocation matching.
5. Fair Pay-As-You-Go Token Economy: No retainers, no contracts, no forced
   rebills. Pay only for what you use using integer tokens that never expire.

3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & KEY SUBSYSTEMS
================================================================================

3.1 COMPETITOR AD INTELLIGENCE ENGINE (RADAR SEARCH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real Ads Only: Strict anti-hallucination policy. Search queries pull real ads
  exclusively from Meta Ad Library, TikTok Commercial Content Library, and
  Google Ads Transparency Center via SearchApi.io with platform isolation.
- Search Flexibility: Search by competitor brand name, product keyword, market
  niche, or specific hook phrase (e.g., "stop scrolling", "the secret to").
- Longevity & Winner Filters:
  * Proven (28+ Days): Ads that have run for at least 4 continuous weeks.
  * Extended (60+ Days): Evergreen powerhouse ads running for two months or more.
  * Top Winners (90%+): Ads achieving top-tier composite Winner Matrix scores.
- Winner Matrix Scoring (0 - 100%):
  * 40% Brand Fit: Alignment with your active brand profile and niche.
  * 30% Hook Strength: Power and uniqueness of the opening 3-second hook.
  * 30% Longevity & Scale: Number of days active, duplicate ad sets, and volume.
- SearchMix Download: Download complete search results and postcard collections
  as structured .txt files directly to your device.

3.2 AI CREATIVE REMIX STUDIO (GEMINI POWERED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Deconstructed Anatomy:
  * Hook Analysis: Psychological triggers and hook angle classification.
  * Pacing & Structure: Second-by-second pacing, visual cut cadence, and flow.
  * Persuasion Mechanics: Problem-Agitation-Solution, social proof, risk reversal.
  * Conversion Blueprint: Why the ad converts and how it maintains viewer attention.
  * Exportable .txt file: Download <brandname>deconstruct.txt instantly.
- 3 Ready-to-Test Creative Variations:
  * Variation A: Direct Response / High Urgency angle.
  * Variation B: Storytelling / Empathy & Social Proof angle.
  * Variation C: Contrarian / Pattern-Interrupt angle.
  * Complete Deliverables: Primary text, headline, CTA button, shot-by-shot
    storyboard with visual cues, voiceover script, and AI image prompts.
- Regenerate Variations: 1-click re-generation to explore fresh creative angles.

3.3 MULTI-BRAND VAULT & BRAND INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Brand Profiles: Manage multiple distinct brand identities under one account.
- Deep Brand Attributes: Name, industry, target customer persona, brand voice
  and tone, unique selling propositions (USPs), pain points, and website URL.
- Brand Clone Capability: Instantly duplicate existing brand profiles to test
  sub-brands, alternative target audiences, or new product lines.
- Brand .txt Export: Download complete brand strategy dossiers as text files.

3.4 AD AGENT MARKETPLACE & GEOLOCATION DISPATCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Independent Contractor Network: Certified Ad Agents provide bespoke brand
  strategy packages, campaign consulting, graphic design, and video production.
- Geolocation Proximity Matching: Haversine distance algorithm calculates the
  closest active Ad Agent based on the customer's suburb, state, and country.
- Customer Choice: When multiple Ad Agents are active, customers can choose
  their preferred agent or default to the closest verified specialist.
- Ad Agent Portal:
  * Self-service status toggle: Turn availability ON or OFF with 1 click.
  * Assigned Orders Queue: Review customer strategy briefs and brand data.
  * AI Strategy Draft Assistant: Generates structured campaign packages.
  * Contractors Directory: Manage specialized subcontractors (video editors,
    graphic artists, animators, copywriters) with PayID/banking notes and
    average rates, inserting their services into client deliverables with 1 click.
- Super Admin Ad Agent & Customizations Value Ledger:
  * Super Admin sets redistribution percentages (10% to 70%) per Ad Agent.
  * Real-time tracking of completed customizations, total tokens, AUD, and USD.
  * ATO-Compliant Payout Invoicing: 1-click download of .txt payout invoices.
  * Reset to Zero Workflow: Clicking "Reset 0 ⚡" zeros out the redistribution
    balance upon settlement, accruing only new orders completed thereafter.
- Ad Agent "Distributions" Tab:
  * Dedicated panel visible exclusively to approved Ad Agents in both their
    Supporter Admin Panel and Ad Agent Panel.
  * Displays Ad Agent Name, Redistribution Percentage, Customizations Total,
    and live Redistribution Amount synced in real-time with Super Admin.

3.5 TOKEN ECONOMY & REVENUE GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Strict Integer Accounting: All token values are strict whole numbers
  (Math.round, non-negative enforcement: tokenBalance >= 0).
- Dynamic Pricing & Live Currency Conversion:
  * Default rate: 1 Token = $1.00 AUD.
  * Real-time exchange rate sync with European Central Bank (ECB) API.
  * Super Admin toggles: isAudEnabled, isUsdEnabled, isTokenSystemEnabled.
  * Geolocation currency display: AUD for Australian users, USD internationally.
  * Full hover tooltips showing exact AUD and USD pricing estimates.
- Zero-Balance Gating: No negative balances permitted. When an account has 0
  tokens, paid operations are blocked until additional tokens are acquired.
- Two Independent Financial Ledgers:
  1. Equity Partner Redistributive Ledger: Aggregates Stripe card purchases
     and distributes revenue to equity partners based on cap table allocations.
  2. Ad Agent Distribution Ledger: Tracks customization revenue sharing for
     Ad Agents based on service completions.
- Free Access Window: Super Admin timed toggle (15, 30, or 60 minutes) for
  monthly subscribers in selected world countries with global countdown banner.

3.6 ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) & SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 6 Permissioned Roles:
  * Super Admin (superadmin): Full platform control, system config, ledger resets,
    user management, role assignment, and financial oversight.
  * Admin (admin): Platform administration, content moderation, user support.
  * Moderator (moderator): Community guidelines enforcement and ticket review.
  * Agency (agency): Multi-client management and shared asset collaboration.
  * Ad Agent (ad_agent): Contractor operations, customize order fulfillment,
    contractors directory, and the exclusive "Distributions" tab.
  * Supporter (supporter): Standard customer user with wallet, search, remix,
    brand vault, and self-service token refresh.
- Role Simulation: Super Admin can preview the application UI as any role
  (Supporter, Ad Agent, Agency, Moderator, Admin) without losing master access.
- User Deletion Safety: 2-step confirmation ("Delete" -> "Confirm Delete"),
  protecting the root Super Admin and caller account while safely removing Auth
  and Firestore documents and preserving statutory 7-year audit records.
- 71-Rule Firestore Security Suite: Strict server-side access control with
  100% automated test coverage in firestore.rules.

3.7 MULTI-LINGUAL RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE & PWA APP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Multi-Lingual RAG Engine: FAQ system supports 30+ languages (English, Chinese,
  Vietnamese, Indonesian, Thai, Tagalog, Malay, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, etc.).
- Google Translate Integration: Instant translation with human-in-the-loop
  verification before publishing to global corpus.
- Universal Progressive Web App (PWA):
  * Standalone fullscreen experience on desktop, Android, and iOS.
  * Service worker static asset caching for offline resilience.
  * 1-click install button for desktop Chrome, custom prompt for Android,
    and 3-step visual walkthrough for Apple Safari/iOS.
